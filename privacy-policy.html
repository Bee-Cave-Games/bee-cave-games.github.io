Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers the games published by Bee Cave Games: Pixel Conquest and Basketball Catch (each, the "Game").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We do not collect personally identifiable information about you, such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The Games have no accounts and no sign-in, and your game progress is stored only on your device.

1.2 Non-Personal Information

The Games show advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners. We do not collect any other data. To deliver and measure ads, these partners may collect information from your device, such as its advertising identifier, IP address (which can indicate your general location), device type and operating system, and how you interact with the ads.

  • Pixel Conquest: advertisements are provided by Google AdMob. The data types collected can be found here: https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Basketball Catch: advertisements are provided by Unity Ads. The data types collected can be found here: https://docs.unity.com/en-us/grow/ads/privacy/google-data-safety

1.3 Your Choices

  • On iOS, you can allow or refuse tracking for each Game when asked, and change it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you refuse, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites.
  • Where the law requires it, the Game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Game.
  • To monitor and analyze usage patterns and make necessary improvements.
  • To show advertisements in the Game.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties, except as described in this Privacy Policy.

  • Third-Party Service Providers: We may share information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating and improving the Game. The advertising partners named in section 1.2 collect the information described there directly from your device.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information when required by law or when we believe it is necessary to protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, or that of our users or the public.

4. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. However, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the revised Privacy Policy on our website or through the Game.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at beecavecontactatx@gmail.com.
